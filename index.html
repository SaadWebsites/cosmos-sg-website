FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Wholesale & International Trade FAQ

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for cashew nuts? +

Our standard MOQ for maritime export is one 20ft Full Container Load (FCL), which accommodates approximately 16.0 Metric Tons (640 cartons of 25kg vacuum packs). For trial orders or specialized single grades (e.g., W180 Jumbo), we accommodate mixed container loads (LCL or mixed FCL) upon prior agreement with our trading desk.

Can we request a sample batch before placing a commercial contract? +

Yes. We provide 500g to 1kg vacuum-sealed laboratory test samples for bona fide corporate buyers and food manufacturers. Samples are dispatched via DHL/FedEx Express alongside full analytical specs (moisture, count, broken percentage).

What payment and trade financing terms does COSMOS SG accept? +

We accept 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit at Sight (L/C at Sight) issued by prime international banks, Telegraphic Transfer (T/T with advance deposit and balance against copy shipping documents), and CAD (Cash Against Documents). All transactions clear securely through our Singapore corporate banking facilities.

How do you guarantee quality and prevent sea damage during transit? +

We utilize nitrogen-flushed multi-barrier vacuum pouches within heavy 5-ply export master cartons. Every container is lined with container desiccants (dry bags) and undergo third-party pre-shipment inspection by SGS or Cotecna for moisture (<5%), count, and weight certification.
